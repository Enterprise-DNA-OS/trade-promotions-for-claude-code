---
description: Every list price increase you notified, and the retailer's answer.
---

Run: `npm run trade -- price-increases`

For Australian customers covered by the code, the answer is due in writing within 30 days (s40(2)).

Options use --name=value. Add --json for structured output. Money is GST exclusive, in each customer's currency. Drafts go to drafts/ and are never sent from here. Nothing here pays, invoices or emails a retailer.
