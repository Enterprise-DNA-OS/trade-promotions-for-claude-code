description: Record the date the retailer's written notice of a funded promotion arrived.
---

Run: `npm run trade -- notice <promotion> --date= --actor=`

Say how many days ahead of store it came, against notice_days in /policy.

Options use --name=value. Add --json for structured output. Money is GST exclusive, in each customer's currency. Drafts go to drafts/ and are never sent from here. Nothing here pays, invoices or emails a retailer.
