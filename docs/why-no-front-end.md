# Trade promotions without a front end

A trade marketing week is a handful of questions: what is in store and what is not yet agreed, which retailer claims and deductions are still to clear, what we owe on promotions that have run, what the last promotions returned, and where a retailer has stepped outside the grocery code. Each of those is one query against a database. Claude Code runs it when you ask in plain words.

What a screen gives that this does not: a drag-and-drop promotion calendar, a planning grid where a key account manager types volumes across 52 weeks, live links to retailer portals, and a forecasting model that sets the baseline for you. The free version has none of those. Account managers add promotions and deal lines by asking, scan data and claims come in as the CSVs the retailers already send, and the baseline is a weekly number per customer and product. Read-only HTML views (`npm run view`) and branded promotion agreements, claim queries, accrual schedules and post-event reports (`npm run docs`) cover what people need to see.

If your team needs a planning grid, a retailer portal feed or a link to your accounting system, Enterprise DNA builds them into your version. The database stays yours either way.
