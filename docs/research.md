# Promax target research

Checked 14 October 2026. New registry family: trade promotion management. Industry: manufacturers.

Annual bill: Wipro Promax (TPM and TPx) publishes no prices. Capterra Australia lists it as priced per feature ([capterra.com.au/software/172090/wipro-promax](https://www.capterra.com.au/software/172090/wipro-promax)). Wipro's own case study describes an Australian food and beverage supplier with about 1,000 staff moving from spreadsheets to Promax Advanced for annual planning, budgeting, promotions, approval workflow and settlement, and says Promax has an extensive presence in food and beverage companies in Australia and New Zealand ([wipro.com case study](https://www.wipro.com/consumer-packaged-goods/case-study/integrating-the-trade-promotion-tools-of-a-food-and-beverage-leader/)). Licence plus a Wipro or partner implementation for a mid-size supplier is typically six figures a year all in; that is an estimate, not an invoice, and the page states no figure. Rivals checked: Salesforce Consumer Goods Cloud lists Trade Promotion Management as contact sales on its add-ons page ([salesforce.com/consumer-goods/pricing/add-ons](https://www.salesforce.com/consumer-goods/pricing/add-ons/)); Vividly lists its plans without prices.

Scores: annual bill 4, data-shaped 5, buyer fit 4, search demand 2, one-session buildable 4, import path 3. Total 22/30.

The rules: the Australian Food and Grocery Code is mandatory from 1 April 2025 (Competition and Consumer (Industry Codes, Food and Grocery) Regulations 2024, F2024L01651): s22 set-offs, s28 funding promotions, s33 funded promotions (written notice, transparent order quantity, no over-ordering, the 10% order cut rule), s40 price increases (written answer within 30 days), s78 records. The New Zealand Grocery Supply Code (Commerce Commission): cl 12 set-offs, cl 17 funding promotions, cl 18 funded promotions after the 2025 review. Details and links: [compliance.md](compliance.md).

The scope is the supplier's promotion cycle: customers and their supply agreement terms, products with list price, cost, shelf price and a weekly baseline per customer, promotions with deal lines (off-invoice, scan, fixed), retailer orders and cuts, scan data, claims and deductions matched to promotions, accruals, post-event uplift and ROI, trade budgets, and price increases. Not in scope: retailer portal links, a forecasting model, payments, or a planning grid.
